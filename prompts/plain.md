Explain $ARGUMENTS to a smart non-specialist. If the subject is unclear, ask what to explain.

Use everyday language and one concrete example where it helps. Define necessary jargon briefly rather than removing terms the reader needs to know. Keep it concise and respectful; never imply the subject is easy or the reader is uninformed. Prefer accuracy over a catchy analogy, and state important caveats or safety warnings plainly.
