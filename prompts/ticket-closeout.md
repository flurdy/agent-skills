description: Assess whole-ticket completion with requirements, deviations and delivery evidence
argument-hint: "<JIRA-key>"
---
Assess whether $ARGUMENTS is ready for Jira Done, not merely whether its latest PR can merge.
Read and follow the `outstanding-work` skill in `ticket-wide` mode for one explicit Jira key.
If the key is missing or ambiguous, ask rather than choosing a ticket.
Use its bounded source ledger, requirement matrix, deviation approvals, owner-routed tracking
inventory and separate PR/ticket/delivery conclusions. Reuse the `verify-task` scope and coverage
rubric as that mode specifies; default to report-only, without running tests.
Do not edit, assign, transition, comment, close, merge, push, install, change credentials or start
follow-up work. Required unknown evidence means incomplete, not clear.
